# Moodboard

The board itself can live in Figma, Are.na, Milanote or on the wall. This file
is its index, so the brief and the machine can read what is on it.

Other people's images stay where they are: link them, credit them, and say why
each one is here. Only your own work and your own photos go in `images/`, as
compressed JPG, PNG, WebP or SVG.

decoration; cut it or say which question it raises.
