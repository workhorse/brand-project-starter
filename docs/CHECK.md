# CHECK.md

What to look for before any writing or screen leaves this repo. These are the
patterns that make work read as machine-made. AI tools produce them by
default, so check everything a tool wrote, and check your own writing too.

Your brand's `voice.json` can overrule a rule here for brand copy. It cannot
overrule the two marked **always**.

## Writing

| Rule | Instead of | Write |
|---|---|---|
| **Always:** no em dashes, no en dashes in sentences | The app is simple — anyone can use it. | The app is simple enough for anyone to use. |
| Vary sentence length | It is fast. It is simple. It works. | It is fast, and simple enough that a first-time user finishes setup alone. |
| Break groups of three same-shaped phrases | Faster. Smarter. Better. | Setup takes two minutes, down from twenty. |
| Say what the thing is, not what it is not | This is not a tool. It is a movement. | Eleven clinics use it to book follow-up visits. |
| No "not just X, it is Y" | It is not just an app, it is a lifestyle. | Say what it does for whom. |
| No reveal lines ("That is the point.") | That repetition is the strategy. | The same header repeats on every page, so new pages cost nothing to design. |
| No motivational closers | The future belongs to the bold. | End on a fact, or stop. |
| Cut throat-clearing | It is worth noting that users skip onboarding. | Users skip onboarding. |
| Cut filler transitions | That said, moreover, furthermore, in other words | Delete them and read it again. |
| Cut filler warmth | At the end of the day, the reality is | Delete them and read it again. |
| Round numbers the way people do | 73.4% of users, 41% within 12 seconds | Most users, and nearly half within seconds |
| Do not tell the reader you are honest | Here is the real story. | Show the thing. |
| Cut what the reader already knows | Branding helps companies stand out. | Start with what is specific to this product. |
| No borrowed years | We have been obsessed with this for a decade. | Say what was done and when. |

## Screens and pages

| Rule | Instead of | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Always:** text passes contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text) | Light gray text on white | Check it with a contrast checker before you commit |
| No colored bar on the edge of a card | A card with a thick left border in the accent color | Type size and space do the emphasis |
| Callout boxes only when type and space cannot do it | A tinted box with an icon around one sentence | The sentence, larger, with room around it |
| No gradient cards without a reason | A diagonal gradient behind every card | A flat surface color from the tokens |
| No emoji as icons | A rocket emoji next to "Fast setup" | A real icon set, or no icon |
| No grid of identical cards | Three equal cards, icon + heading + two lines | A list, or make the most important item bigger |
| Few badges | A status pill, category pill and date pill on every row | One label where the reader has to tell things apart |
| Every section says something new | A band that repeats the section above | Cut it, or give it the evidence the section above only claimed |
| No baked 3D mockups | A rendered book or phone at an angle | Flat artwork; add shadow in the layout |
| Photos at their own shape, with a caption | Every photo cropped to the same square | Keep the ratio, say what is in the frame |
