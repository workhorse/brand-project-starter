# Check a draft

Read the draft as a stranger. If you wrote it in this session, say so first:
the writer is the worst judge of their own draft.

1. Read `docs/CHECK.md` and `04-brand/voice.json`.
2. Go through the draft rule by rule. For each hit, quote the words, name the
   rule, and give a rewrite.
3. For screens, also check that every color, size and space comes from
   `05-tokens/tokens.css` and not from a typed value.
4. Report the hits, most serious first. Do not rewrite the whole draft unless
   asked; the student decides what to take.
