# 02-research

What you found out, and where you found it. One file per kind of research:

- `audiences.md`: your three audiences and the lean (from the audience form).
- `competitors.md`: what people use today instead, and what it costs them.
- `sources.md`: every source, one line each, with a link and the date you read it.
- Interview notes go in `interviews/YYYY-MM-DD-name.md`. Ask before you name a person.

A claim with no source gets a `?` at the end of the line. AI tools may help
you search and summarize; they may not supply a fact without a source you
opened yourself.
